Questions

Straight answers.

Will applying hurt my credit?
No. Seeing your offer takes a soft pull only, which never affects your score. A hard inquiry happens only if you accept an offer on certain products — and I'll tell you before it does.
How fast can I actually get the money?
Rapid response in as little as 2 hours, and 95% of approved clients are funded within 48 hours of approval. Same-day wires happen regularly when applications come in before early afternoon with statements attached.
What do you need from me?
A one-page application and your last 3 business bank statements. That's it to see your offer. If you accept, funding usually needs a driver's license, a voided business check, and an e-signature.
What will it cost?
Pricing is per file — it depends on your revenue, time in business, and credit, with rates starting as low as 1% per month for well-qualified files.* You'll see your exact total cost in writing before you sign anything, and if the math doesn't make sense for your business, I'll tell you so.
I was declined before. Should I bother?
Yes. Declines are usually about timing — a slow month, a high balance on an existing advance. I re-run files after 45 days and regularly fund businesses other shops passed on.
I already have a loan or advance.
That often helps, not hurts. If you're past roughly halfway paid down, you likely qualify for additional capital or a consolidation that lowers your total payment.
