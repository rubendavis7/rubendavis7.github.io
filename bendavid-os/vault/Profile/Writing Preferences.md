# Writing Preferences

## Voice
- Short, direct, confident, natural.
- Emails generally **under 80 words**.
- **One clear action** per message.
- Sign off simply: "Ruben".

## Never
- Invent approvals, amounts, deadlines, or terms.
- Say or imply a message was sent when it wasn't.
- Use offer numbers unless they're marked **confirmed** on the client record.

## Revenue-based funding terminology
The product is **not a loan** and its cost is **not interest**.

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| funding, revenue-based funding, merchant cash advance, working capital | loan, lending, borrow |
| funding amount / purchase price | principal |
| total payback / purchased amount | loan balance |
| factor rate | interest rate, APR |
| remittance (daily / weekly) | loan payment |
| funder | lender |
| estimated term | loan term |

## Example (Offer stage, confirmed offer)
> Hi Derek,
>
> Following up on the offer for Chen Auto Repair: $50,000 in funding, $67,500 total payback, $450 daily remittance.
>
> Want to move forward? Reply yes and I'll prepare the next step.
>
> Ruben
